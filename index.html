AKINYI OMONDI
PORTFOLIO

Akinyi builds the models behind how central banks across Africa assess credit risk.

As a Quantitative Analyst and Data Scientist at Innova Limited, she helped develop a credit scoring tool now used by 14 central banks and led its deployment for one of them. Her work spans statistical modelling, machine learning, NLP and the data pipelines and dashboards that turn raw financial data into decisions.

She is completing a Master of Economics (Finance) at Kenyatta University and has completed the ALX Data Science program. Beyond the code, she leads Innova's women-in-tech initiative which has led to more women taking up senior management roles and is committed to using data to drive impactful, equitable decisions.

With Machine Learning

This project uses machine learning to identify retail transactions at risk of being unprofitable. After preparing the data and exploring key drivers of profit, I trained and evaluated predictive models to assess how factors such as discounts, product categories, and order characteristics relate to profitability. The analysis shows how predictive insights can help retailers investigate potential losses and make more informed pricing and sales decisions.

Robust Estimators Research
Implemented With R Programming

The project focused on the implementation of robust statistical estimators using R programming to analyze Safaricom's prices dataset. The goal was to enhance data resilience against outliers and model mis-specifications, ensuring more reliable estimates. By leveraging robust methods, the analysis aimed to improve the accuracy of insights derived from the data, particularly in challenging or non-standard conditions.
